Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

AST TipWise collects no data. The app works fully offline: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no third parties, and no network requests of any kind. There is nothing to opt out of — there is simply nothing collected.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter — tips, budgets, debts, subscriptions, goals — is stored only in on-device storage on your phone. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently removes everything.

Home Vault encryption

The optional Home Vault encrypts your entries on your device with AES-256-GCM. Your PIN is used to derive the encryption key (PBKDF2, 100,000 iterations) and is never stored, transmitted, or recoverable. If you forget your vault PIN, your vault data cannot be recovered — there is no reset.

Home Wallet & app lock

The optional Home Wallet is protected by a local account (username + password) stored only on your device — passwords are kept as PBKDF2 hashes, never in plain text. Your wallet data is encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM. There is no email, no cloud backup, and no way for us to recover your data if you forget your credentials without a recovery question.

Camera & microphone (optional)

The app can use your camera (receipt scanning) and microphone (voice input for transaction notes) — but only if you tap those features. Permission is requested at that moment, never at install, and you can deny it: the rest of the app keeps working. Photos you attach are compressed on-device and stored only in your encrypted wallet data. Voice input is the one feature that needs internet (your device's speech service); everything else is 100% offline.

Update check (optional)

Once a day, the app may download a small public version file to tell you about updates. No personal data is sent — just a plain file download. You can turn this off in Settings.

State tax rates

State sales-tax rates shown in the app are approximate base state rates. County and city taxes vary — always check your receipt for the exact rate.
